For NGOs, Consortia & Subsidy Applicants (RVO/EU)

The Crucial Private Sector Link for Your Grant Application

Maximize the success rate of your RVO or EU subsidy application for Ivorian cashew projects with a proven market partner.

The value chain challenge

One project per country, per cycle — no room for a weak partner.

Dutch and European grant schemes (such as RVO Fair Focus on Trade or SPVO) enforce strict criteria: applications must demonstrate a clear link to the Dutch market alongside proven execution capacity in Côte d'Ivoire. Furthermore, funding bodies often maintain strict rules allowing only one cashew project per country per application cycle. Submitting a proposal without a solid, commercially active private sector partner risks losing out on substantial grant budgets.

Our solution

The Dutch-Ivorian private sector partner your consortium needs.

OrigoVia Partners serves as your committed private sector consortium partner. We provide the necessary Dutch market linkages, Ivorian ground presence, and sector expertise to elevate your application quality and maximize approval probability.
