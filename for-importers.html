Sourcing & Quality Risk Management

Zero-Claim Guarantee on Ivorian Cashews

Guarantee 100% quality compliance and eliminate costly port rejections and reputational damage prior to shipment from Côte d'Ivoire.

The value chain challenge

Every container is capital at risk.

For European and Dutch importers, every container of vacuum-packed cashew kernels originating from Côte d'Ivoire represents significant capital. A single rejection at a European port due to moisture, microbiological contamination, or an inconsistent Kernel Output Ratio (KOR) results in tens of thousands of Euros in direct losses and stalled packaging lines. Furthermore, CSDDD and CSRD mandate strict due diligence over environmental and labor standards; non-compliance can lead to fines of up to 5% of global turnover and severe brand damage.

Our solution

Complete risk mitigation directly at the Ivorian source.

OrigoVia Partners conducts physical quality inspections and ESG audits prior to shipment at the ports of Abidjan or San Pédro. In doing so, we build a transparent, claim-free supply chain to Europe.

Key features & value

What's included in every engagement.

Physical quality audits in Côte d'Ivoire — KOR analysis, moisture testing, grading, and laboratory controls — before container sealing.

ESG & due diligence verification

Independent verification of safe working conditions on the Ivorian factory floor to safeguard your brand reputation.

Direct European reporting

Transparent, objective audit reports delivered directly to your quality and procurement teams in the Netherlands and Europe.
