Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

1. Who we are

OrigoVia Partners ("we", "us", "our") operates this website to present our services connecting Ivorian cashew processors and cooperatives with European importers, investors and institutional partners. This policy explains what personal data we collect through this website and how we use it.

2. What information we collect

We collect information you provide directly, such as your name, organization, email address, and the content of any message you send us through our contact form, email, or WhatsApp. We may also collect basic technical information automatically, such as your browser type and general location, for the purpose of understanding how our website is used.

3. How we use your information

We use the information you provide to respond to your enquiry, schedule meetings, and communicate with you about our services. We do not sell or rent your personal data to third parties. We may share information with service providers who help us operate this website or manage communications, under appropriate confidentiality arrangements.

4. Legal basis for processing

Where applicable under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), we process your data on the basis of your consent when you submit a form or message to us, or on the basis of our legitimate interest in responding to business enquiries.

5. Data retention

We retain personal data only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purpose for which it was collected, or as required by law.

6. Your rights

If you are located in the European Union or the United Kingdom, you have the right to access, correct, or request deletion of your personal data, and to object to or restrict certain processing. To exercise these rights, please contact us using the details on our Contact page.

7. Third-party links

Our website may contain links to third-party services, such as WhatsApp or scheduling tools. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of these third parties, and we encourage you to review their own privacy policies.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

9. Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy or how your data is handled, please reach out via our Contact page.
